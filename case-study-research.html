03 / Published research

What makes a startup valuable?

Looking beyond financial measures to examine qualitative factors in IT/IS startup valuation.

Project type
Published research
Method
Literature review
Published
26 June 2025
Role
First-listed author
01 / Question02 / Approach03 / Contribution04 / Outcome & evidence

The question.

Which qualitative factors influence IT/IS startup valuation, and how are they represented in the existing literature?

A financial view alone may not capture the full context of a young technology venture. This research investigates the qualitative dimensions discussed in startup valuation literature.

The approach.

  • Review existing research on qualitative factors in IT/IS startup valuation.
  • Synthesise the literature into a structured discussion of valuation considerations.
  • Connect the findings with questions relevant to founders, investors, and innovation ecosystems.

The contribution.

Vishal is the first-listed author of the published paper. The publication provides the full methodology, source literature, and findings; individual contributions are not inferred beyond authorship.

What the work shows.

The research was published in Multidisciplinary Reviews in June 2025. The portfolio also includes a certificate for Best Paper, 3rd Place, at ASIP 2024.

A literature review provides a synthesis of evidence. It is not a tested valuation engine or a claim of improved investment returns.
